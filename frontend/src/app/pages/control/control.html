Inverter control

Let the planner set your Fox ESS inverter's overnight force charge for you. It's off unless you turn it on, every change is shown before it's made and checked afterwards, and you can undo it at any time.

Before you turn this on

  • The planner will use your Fox ESS API key to change your inverter's mode schedule: it adds a force charge period for the cheap window, with the charge level, charge power and minimum charge. Your other schedule periods are kept.
  • Fox schedules repeat every day, so a charge stays in place on later nights until it's changed or removed.
  • Nothing is written until you run the write test or press Apply. Automatic nightly apply is a separate choice you can make later.
  • This has not been tested against every inverter. Fox's own app can always see and change the schedule, and you can remove the planner's periods or restore your original schedule here.
