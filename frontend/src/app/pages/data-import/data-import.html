How your data is used

  • Your data is used only to predict your household's usage and solar generation and to recommend how much to charge your battery. It is not shared, sold or used for anything else.
  • Your Fox ESS API key is stored encrypted. By default it is used only to read your inverter's history and settings, and the planner never changes your inverter. If you turn on inverter control (off unless you choose it), the key is also used to write the overnight force charge to your inverter's schedule, each change is logged, and you can undo it or turn control off at any time. Disconnecting Fox ESS or deleting your data turns control off.
  • To look up historical weather, your system's latitude and longitude (nothing else) are sent to Open-Meteo.
  • You can delete your imported data, or your whole account, at any time at the bottom of this page. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Upload a CSV

If you don't use Fox ESS, or want to fill gaps, upload a CSV with a header row and these columns: start, load_kwh, solar_kwh, grid_import_kwh, grid_export_kwh and optionally battery_soc_percent. start is each interval's start time (e.g. 2024-06-01T10:30, read in your system's time zone). Intervals of 30 minutes or less are added up into half hours. Uploading the same period again replaces it.

Delete your data

Delete imported data removes all usage and weather data, your import history and your Fox ESS API key, and keeps your system details. Delete account removes everything, including your login.
